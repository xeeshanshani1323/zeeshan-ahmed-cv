About Me

Dr. Zeeshan Ahmed holds a PhD in Information Technology from Khwaja Fareed University of Engineering and Information Technology (KFUEIT). His academic experience spans Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, databases, computer networks, and bioinformatics.

He serves at Superior University, Rahim Yar Khan, with academic leadership responsibilities including Head of Department, Program Leader CSIT, Quality Circle Head, and Manager International Exchange Program. His research interests include deep learning, computational biology, medical image analysis, cybersecurity, and intelligent data-driven systems.
